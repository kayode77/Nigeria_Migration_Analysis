--------------------------------------------------------------
INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------------

Nigeria is the largest West African source represented in
this dataset.

Nigeria's cumulative share of West African arrivals is
approximately 54.4%.

Nigeria's historical African ranking differs from its
recent ranking. The country has been among the leading
African sources in the most recent complete years.

The large increase between 2022 and 2023 should be treated
as an important structural change requiring further
investigation.

The regression results demonstrate statistical association
with time and the post-2022 intervention period. They do
not establish the cause of the change.

Correlation measures association rather than causation.

The Holt-Winters result is a statistical benchmark rather
than an official New Zealand government forecast.


--------------------------------------------------------------
LIMITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------

1. Country of last permanent residence is not necessarily
   the same as citizenship.

2. Migration arrivals are a flow, not a population stock.

3. Net migration requires arrivals and departures.

4. March 2026 is incomplete.

5. Migration estimates may be revised.

6. Statistical association does not establish causality.

7. Regional classification depends on the country categories
   represented in the supplied dataset.
