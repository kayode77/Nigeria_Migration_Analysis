This report delivers an empirical analysis of Nigeria's international migration status within both West Africa and the broader African
continent based on official long-term migration estimates (2001–2026). Nigeria stands as the undisputed primary origin of
migration in West Africa, generating 54.4% of all long-term migrant arrivals from the ECOWAS region. Pan-Africanly, Nigeria
ranks 6th overall in total arrivals, behind South Africa, Zimbabwe, Egypt, Kenya, and Ethiopia. Migration from Nigeria is
predominantly driven by tertiary education (Student Visas: 32.1%) and skilled labor (Work Visas: 21.5%), with annual outflows
surging by over 200% post-2022 under the socio-economic 'Japa' phenomenon.
